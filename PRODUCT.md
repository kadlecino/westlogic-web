## Product Purpose

Corporate website of Westlogic s.r.o., a Czech e-commerce group from Opava that creates its own brands and builds them into market leaders in fast-growing niches (creative hobby products, interior shading, AI). Success: a visitor understands what the group does, sees the brand portfolio and its reach, and makes contact.

## Positioning

Westlogic builds its own brands from scratch instead of reselling. It uses data analysis and AI to find high-growth categories, then controls product development, own production (Staré Město), marketing and customer care in-house. Two of its brands claim European leadership in their category.

## Operating Context

- Sells mainly online, some offline stores, across 12 European markets: CZ, DE, AT, FR, UK, ES, NL, PL, SK, RO, BG, HU.
- Three sites: HQ Opava (Olomoucká 267/29, 746 01), branch Ostrava (Hornopolní 3314/38, 702 00), production Staré Město (Na Zbytkách 158, 739 01).
- Languages: Czech (primary), English.
